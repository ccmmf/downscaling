CARB Downscaling Review Draft

Inventory, practice change effects, and scenarios

Author

David LeBauer

Published

September 27, 2026

Purpose

This draft assembles the downscaling evidence available through September 27, 2026 into a short review path for Akash. It covers the 2016–2023 historical inventory, a separate paired analysis of practice change effects, and four 2024–2045 scenario configurations. The narrative and summary tables are intended to support CARB milestone review and later reuse in the final report.

The model has been minimally tested against site level observations and syntheses of available research. The execution, completeness, and aggregation checks documented here establish that the reported products were generated and reconciled as described; they do not by themselves establish agreement with observations.

Three distinct products

Product Question answered Evidence in this draft Interpretation boundary
Inventory What model-derived stocks and flux rates result from recorded historical inputs? January 2016–December 2023 results for 385,234 fields, summarized by county, state, crop class, and 20 ensemble members Start-to-end changes are historical inventory trajectories, not causal practice effects
Practice change effects How do paired practice and control simulations differ at the calibration design sites? Design summary and links to calibration PR #6 and its rendered report 99 paired sites and one fixed parameterization; not an ensemble or a statewide practice-adoption estimate
Scenarios How do management, climate-model, and emissions-pathway choices change future model results? Four 2024–2045 configurations; SOC for all four; annual native-N accounting for reference and management; bounded CH4 evidence Each arm has its own successful simulation mask; gas evidence is not yet complete across all arms

Evidence available for review

The historical inventory uses 1,000 SIPNET design sites and 20 original ensemble-member identities. It covers 385,234 annual and woody-perennial crop fields and 3,485,601.6801 ha, or 98.83% of classified crop area. Another 9,679 fields were excluded because the required covariates were absent. The inventory overview, summary tables, and variable-specific atlas pages provide the report-ready results.

The scenario delivery contains 76,998 successful simulations and 1,542 explicit failures across the four configurations. Each configuration was downscaled to the same 385,234 covered fields while retaining all 20 member identifiers. The scenario report presents the definitions, support counts, SOC responses, annual reference-versus-management native-N accounting, and bounded methane diagnostic.

The existing training overview, environment setup, and inventory walkthrough remain the training sources. They are linked here rather than duplicated.
