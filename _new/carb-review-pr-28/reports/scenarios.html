Management, climate-model, and emissions-pathway comparisons

Author

David LeBauer

Published

Overview

The four configurations isolate a management comparison, a climate-model comparison, and an emissions-pathway comparison against the same reference definition. Results cover January 2024 through December 2045.

Configuration Management Climate model Pathway Comparison with reference
Reference BAU CESM2 SSP370 Baseline for the three contrasts
Management NBS CESM2 SSP370 NBS minus BAU
Model BAU MPI-ESM1-2-HR SSP370 MPI minus CESM2
Pathway BAU CESM2 SSP585 SSP585 minus SSP370

Here, BAU and NBS are management-input definitions. They are not labels for the historical inventory or the separate paired practice change effects analysis.

Soil carbon response

Soil carbon response is the December 2045 monthly mean minus the January 2024 monthly mean. A negative value indicates lower modeled soil carbon at the end of the window; it is not an annual rate. The summaries first aggregate field stocks within each original member and then describe the distribution across the 20 members.

Configuration Mean change (Tg C) Member p05 (Tg C) Member p95 (Tg C) Mean change (Mg C / ha) Members below / above zero
Reference -45.33 -98.26 17.87 -13.01 16 / 4
Management -43.77 -97.21 20.86 -12.56 16 / 4
Model -45.74 -95.55 15.98 -13.12 17 / 3
Pathway -49.04 -101.24 11.96 -14.07 17 / 3

The p05 and p95 values describe empirical spread across the original members, not observational confidence intervals.

Contrast Mean contrast (Tg C) Member p05 (Tg C) Member p95 (Tg C) Mean contrast (Mg C / ha)
Management minus reference 1.55 -1.81 3.86 0.45
Model minus reference -0.41 -5.06 3.84 -0.12
Pathway minus reference -3.71 -6.16 -1.31 -1.07

The management contrast is positive on average even though the management and reference arms both have negative mean statewide stock changes. This means the NBS configuration retains more modeled soil carbon than BAU over the window; it does not mean statewide stocks increase relative to January 2024.

Annual model-defined N2O-N

The annual series integrates model-defined N2O-N over each calendar year before downscaling. Native units are kg N and kg N ha-1 per calendar year. The available audited series covers the reference and management configurations; the model and pathway annual gas series are not included. Molecular N2O values can be derived with 44/28, but no CO2-equivalent balance is calculated here.

Year Reference (kg N / ha) Management (kg N / ha) Management - reference (kg N / ha)

A positive contrast means higher modeled N2O-N under management than reference.

Bounded methane evidence

The current methane evidence is a deliberately bounded diagnostic: one reference simulation (ENS-00005-130284), segment 004, integrated from March 5 through October 3, 2028. The result is 0.01036 kg CH4-C ha-1, or 0.01382 kg CH4 ha-1. It is neither a statewide estimate nor a recurring annual series, and there is no paired management effect in this diagnostic.

The original scenario gas endpoint outputs are annualized January 2024 and December 2045 monthly mean rates. They should not be interpreted as integrated annual or cumulative emissions. Completed statewide annual and cumulative CH4 accounting remains follow-up work.
