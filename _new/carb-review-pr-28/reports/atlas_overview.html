Downscaling Inventory Overview

Carbon and GHG inventory for annual and woody crops

Author

Akash B V

Published

September 27, 2026

Summary

The inventory uses 1,000 SIPNET sites and 20 ensemble members to predict four variables for 385,234 annual and woody-perennial fields, representing 98.83% of classified crop area. Endpoints are December 2023 monthly means; changes subtract January 2016 monthly means. Flux endpoints are annualized mean rates, not integrated annual emissions.

Stocks and flux rates describe different quantities and should be read in their own units. The annual and woody crop strata are reported separately. Spatial model diagnostics measure agreement with SIPNET outputs, not observed field outcomes.

Practice change effects

Practice change effects are available in the calibration report and calibration PR #6. That analysis compares paired practice and control simulations at 99 design sites using one fixed parameterization over 2016–2023. It is separate from both this historical inventory and the 2024–2045 scenarios.

Methods overview

SIPNET outputs at the 1,000 frozen design sites are downscaled separately for annual and woody crops, preserving all 20 members. Field predictions are multiplied by field area and summed within each member before calculating county or state ensemble summaries. The models use 28 numeric predictors; identifiers and climate-region identifiers are excluded.

The historical inventory is labeled baseline by the CLI. Management inputs follow the recorded crop histories, including documented date repairs; this label does not imply uniform tillage, fertilizer, or irrigation across fields.

Simulation period: 2016–2023. The endpoint is December 2023; temporal change is December 2023 minus January 2016.

Model outputs:

  • TotSoilCarb — modeled total soil carbon; a fixed 30 cm interpretation is not established by the reader.
  • AGB — aboveground biomass carbon, not dry biomass or harvested yield.
  • N₂O flux — annualized endpoint rate; source units are kg N ha⁻¹ yr⁻¹.
  • CH₄ flux — annualized endpoint rate; source units are kg C ha⁻¹ yr⁻¹.

Result files and figures remain in the NCSA production directory documented in scope, units, and data locations.
