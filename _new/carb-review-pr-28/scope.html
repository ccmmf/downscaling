Scope, Units, and Data Locations

Author

David LeBauer

Published

September 27, 2026

Analytical scope

This package keeps three analytical products distinct.

Product Spatial and temporal scope Primary comparison
Historical inventory 385,234 covered fields; county and state summaries; January 2016–December 2023 December 2023 endpoint and December 2023 minus January 2016 change under recorded historical inputs
Practice change effects 99 paired calibration design sites; 2016–2023; one fixed parameterization Practice minus control within the calibration analysis
Future scenarios 385,234 covered fields; January 2024–December 2045; 20 original member identities Management, climate-model, and pathway configurations relative to the reference configuration

The inventory and scenario deliveries cover 3,485,601.6801 ha. Another 9,679 classified crop fields, representing 41,260.9767 ha, were excluded because required covariates were absent. The reported values are therefore covered-area results, not complete California totals.

Variables, estimands, and units

Result Native quantity and unit Time basis Interpretation
Inventory TotSoilCarb Mg C; Mg C ha-1 January 2016 and December 2023 monthly means Carbon stock; endpoint difference is not an annual rate or a causal practice effect
Inventory AGB Mg C; Mg C ha-1 January 2016 and December 2023 monthly means Standing aboveground biomass carbon, not dry biomass or harvested yield
Inventory N2O flux kg N yr-1; kg N ha-1 yr-1 Annualized monthly endpoint rate Source tables retain N mass; maps use N2O mass after multiplying by 44/28; not integrated annual emissions
Inventory CH4 flux kg C yr-1; kg C ha-1 yr-1 Annualized monthly endpoint rate Source tables retain C mass; maps use CH4 mass after multiplying by 16/12; not integrated annual emissions
Scenario soil carbon Mg C, Tg C, and Mg C ha-1 December 2045 monthly mean minus January 2024 monthly mean Stock response over the full window; not an annual SOC series
Scenario annual N kg N and kg N ha-1 per calendar year Monthly model-defined N2O-N integrated within each year Audited statewide series currently covers reference and management only
Bounded scenario CH4 kg CH4-C ha-1 and kg CH4 ha-1 One site/member crop window in 2028 Scale diagnostic only; not statewide or a recurring annual series

No combined CO2-equivalent balance is reported. The available member p05 and p95 values describe empirical spread across the 20 original members; they are not observational confidence intervals or complete uncertainty bounds.

Ensemble identity and comparison support

Inventory fields are summed within each original ensemble member before state or county means and SDs are calculated. This preserves the dependence among fields generated from the same member.

The four scenario arms retain the same 20 member identifiers but have different successful site/member masks. Contrasts use the original member identity and common successful support: 19,115 pairs for management minus reference and 19,252 pairs for each of model minus reference and pathway minus reference. Because the arms were fitted independently, these are not matched-pair downscaling refits.

Input and interpretation boundaries

  • The frozen historical ensemble remains conditional on the recorded management inputs. An upstream planting parcel-assignment issue is unresolved; repairing missing dates does not establish that every date was assigned to the correct parcel.
  • The full inventory pipeline passed row-count, key, finiteness, endpoint, and field-to-county-to-state reconciliation checks. Those checks establish computational consistency, not agreement with observations.
  • Original scenario gas outputs are January 2024 and December 2045 annualized endpoint rates. Only the reference and management annual native-N series has been assembled and audited for this draft.
  • The bounded CH4 result cannot be extrapolated to fields, counties, the state, other members, or other years.
  • Practice change effects are reported in calibration PR #6 and its rendered report. They are not statewide downscaled adoption benefits.

Verification summary

Historical inventory validation accounted for 1,000 sites, 20 members, 7,680,000 monthly records, 385,234 predicted fields, and 30,818,720 rows in each field-level endpoint product. County and state totals reconciled with field area-weighted values at 1e-10 relative tolerance.

Scenario delivery validation recorded 76,998 successful simulations and 1,542 explicit failures. Each of the four downscaled configurations contains 30,818,720 prediction rows and 30,818,720 endpoint-difference rows.
