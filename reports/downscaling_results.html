California Agricultural Carbon and Greenhouse Gas Results

Historical Inventory and Future Scenarios

Authors

David LeBauer

Akash B V

Published

September 28, 2026

This report summarizes the historical downscaling inventory and the future management and climate configurations. The historical inventory describes modeled conditions from January 2016 through December 2023. The scenario analysis compares four configurations from January 2024 through December 2045.

Historical inventory summary

The inventory covers annual crops and woody perennial crops under recorded historical inputs. Stock values are December 2023 monthly means. Nitrous-oxide and methane values are December 2023 annualized endpoint rates, not emissions integrated over 2016–2023.

Soil carbon is the largest reported carbon pool. County totals reflect both the modeled stock density and the agricultural area represented within each county; field-density maps separate the per-hectare pattern from area. Woody perennial crops contain much more standing aboveground biomass than annual crops at the December endpoint.

The inventory maps indicate net soil-carbon declines over much of the represented area from 2016 to 2023. These changes are stock differences between endpoint monthly means. They are not annualized sequestration rates.

Nitrous-oxide results are reported in native nitrogen units. The maps show annualized December 2023 rates and changes in that endpoint rate. They should not be added directly to soil-carbon stock changes.

Future scenario summary

All four future configurations have a negative mean statewide soil-carbon response between January 2024 and December 2045. The management configuration has a mean response 1.55 Tg C higher than the reference, indicating less modeled stock loss rather than a net statewide stock gain. The SSP585 configuration has a mean response 3.71 Tg C lower than the reference.

Annual model-defined N2O-N is available for the reference and management configurations. The management mean is slightly higher than the reference in most years, although the matched-member spread commonly crosses zero. See Future Management and Climate Scenarios for the full time series and county SOC contrasts.
